[STYLE]
Bright saturated cartoon pixel UI in the same family as a vivid zoo illustration: rich lawn green, clear pond blue, strong sunflower yellow, warm cream, soft flower pink used only as a small accent. Crisp pixel edges, medium grain, clean flat blocks, thin dark-green outlines, high-key light. More saturated than a pale pastel kit. Not glossy, not chocolate-brown, not 8-bit, not gray, not photoreal.

[CAMERA]
flat front view, zero perspective, orthographic, every widget face-on. Not top-down, not isometric.

[LIGHTING]
even soft light. One-pixel darker rim inside each widget. No cast shadows, no glossy highlights.

[ASSETS]
30 separate widgets. Dialog title and dialog body are separate pieces and must not be joined.
- title plank 240x48, cream bar with a lawn-green rim, empty, no letters
- panel 176x168, ONE simple rounded frame in lawn green, ONE flat cream center, no second frame, no header baked in
- resource bar 96x64, green rim, cream center
- item slot 96x96, green square frame, empty cream center
- selection frame 104x104, hollow sunflower-yellow outline, empty center
- count plate 48x32, small green badge, cream center
- primary button 288x80, rich lawn green, empty
- secondary button 288x80, cream face, green rim, empty
- watch-ad button 288x80, sunflower yellow, empty
- gold-pay button 288x80, warm gold, empty
- diamond-pay button 288x80, clear pond blue, empty
- close button 72x72, cream square, green rim, dark X
- help button 72x72, cream square, green rim, dark question mark
- slider track 96x24, thin green groove
- slider thumb 40x40, separate cream knob
- progress fill 96x16, solid lawn-green bar
- nine bare icons, object only, no plate, no frame: gold coin, blue diamond, yellow lightning, music note, speaker, globe, play triangle, plus sign, minus sign
- tab normal 160x80, cream, green rim, empty
- tab selected 160x80, rich lawn green, empty
- small primary button 80x80, lawn green square, empty
- small secondary button 80x80, cream square, green rim, empty
- stepper number plate 128x80, cream, green rim, no digits

[LAYOUT]
1024x1024 canvas. Do not enlarge widgets to fill the sheet. Every piece is isolated.
Left column: the five wide buttons stacked, with a clear gap between each.
Right side, kept far from the buttons:
1. title plank alone at the top
2. a wide empty gap, then the panel below it. The plank and the panel must not touch
3. item slot and selection frame to the right of the panel, each separated
4. close and help further right, separated
5. tab normal, then a large empty gap at least 80 pixels wide, then tab selected. The two tabs must not touch or overlap
6. stepper plate, then the two small squares, all separated
7. slider track, slider thumb, and progress fill as three objects that do not touch
8. the nine icons in one bottom row, each object floating with space around it, no squares behind them

[BACKGROUND]
flat bright cream, one solid color.

[RESTRICTIONS]
no words, no digits, no labels, no watermark, no animals, no people, no buildings, no map. No overlapping widgets. No nested double frame on the panel. Icons have no backing plates.
