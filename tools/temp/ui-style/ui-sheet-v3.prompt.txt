[STYLE]
Same pixel craft as a bright cozy zoo map: lawn green, sand-path cream, pond blue, sunflower yellow, and the pale oak of wooden animal signs and fences. Chubby rounded signboards, visible medium pixel grain, crisp edges, clean color blocks, thin dark-olive outlines. High-key and saturated, not washed out. Not glossy plastic, not thick chocolate frames, not 8-bit, not gray, not photoreal.

[CAMERA]
flat front view, zero perspective, orthographic, every widget face-on. Not top-down, not isometric.

[LIGHTING]
even soft light. One-pixel darker rim inside each widget. No cast shadows, no glossy highlights.

[ASSETS]
30 separate widgets. The dialog is two pieces: a title plank and a body board. They must not be joined.
- title plank 240x48, pale oak bar like a zoo sign header, empty, no letters
- panel 176x168, ONE pale-oak frame and ONE flat cream paper center, like a single animal signboard, no second inner frame, no header baked in
- resource bar 96x64, pale oak rim, cream center
- item slot 96x96, pale oak square frame, empty cream center
- selection frame 104x104, hollow lawn-green outline, empty center
- count plate 48x32, small oak badge, cream center
- primary button 288x80, lawn green, empty
- secondary button 288x80, cream face, pale oak rim, empty
- watch-ad button 288x80, sunflower yellow, empty
- gold-pay button 288x80, warm sand, empty
- diamond-pay button 288x80, pond blue, empty
- close button 72x72, pale oak square, dark X
- help button 72x72, pale oak square, dark question mark
- slider track 96x24, thin oak groove
- slider thumb 40x40, separate cream knob
- progress fill 96x16, solid lawn-green bar
- nine bare icons, object only, no plate, no frame: gold coin, blue diamond, yellow lightning, music note, speaker, globe, play triangle, plus sign, minus sign
- tab normal 160x80, cream sign with pale oak rim, empty
- tab selected 160x80, lawn green, empty
- small primary button 80x80, lawn green square, empty
- small secondary button 80x80, cream square, pale oak rim, empty
- stepper number plate 128x80, cream inset, pale oak rim, no digits

[LAYOUT]
1024x1024 canvas. Do not enlarge widgets to fill the sheet. Every piece is isolated.
Left column: the five wide buttons stacked, with a clear gap between each.
Right side, kept far from the buttons:
1. title plank alone at the top
2. a wide empty gap, then the panel below it. The plank and the panel must not touch
3. item slot and selection frame to the right of the panel, each separated
4. close and help further right, separated
5. tab normal, then a large empty gap at least 80 pixels wide, then tab selected. The two tabs must not touch or overlap
6. stepper plate, then the two small squares, all separated
7. slider track, slider thumb, and progress fill as three objects that do not touch
8. the nine icons in one bottom row, each object floating with space around it, no squares behind them

[BACKGROUND]
flat warm cream, the color of a zoo sand path, one solid color.

[RESTRICTIONS]
no words, no digits, no labels, no watermark, no animals, no people, no buildings, no map. No overlapping widgets. No nested double frame on the panel. Icons have no backing plates.
