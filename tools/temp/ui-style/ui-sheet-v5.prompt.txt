[STYLE]
Cozy zoo-shop pixel UI: cream walls, pale wood trim, and a short green-and-white striped awning used only on the title plank, like a small souvenir stall. Buttons are clean blocks of lawn green, sand gold, pond blue, and sunflower yellow. Medium pixel grain, crisp edges, thin dark-olive outlines, bright and airy. Not glossy plastic, not chocolate-brown, not 8-bit, not gray, not photoreal.

[CAMERA]
flat front view, zero perspective, orthographic, every widget face-on. Not top-down, not isometric.

[LIGHTING]
even soft light. One-pixel darker rim inside each widget. No cast shadows, no glossy highlights.

[ASSETS]
30 separate widgets. The striped awning is only the title plank. The panel below is a plain board and must not wear the awning.
- title plank 240x48, a short green-and-white striped awning bar, empty, no letters
- panel 176x168, ONE pale-wood frame, ONE flat cream center, no stripes, no second frame, no header baked in
- resource bar 96x64, pale wood rim, cream center
- item slot 96x96, pale wood square frame, empty cream center
- selection frame 104x104, hollow lawn-green outline, empty center
- count plate 48x32, small wood badge, cream center
- primary button 288x80, lawn green, empty
- secondary button 288x80, cream face, pale wood rim, empty
- watch-ad button 288x80, sunflower yellow, empty
- gold-pay button 288x80, sand gold, empty
- diamond-pay button 288x80, pond blue, empty
- close button 72x72, pale wood square, dark X
- help button 72x72, pale wood square, dark question mark
- slider track 96x24, thin wood groove
- slider thumb 40x40, separate cream knob
- progress fill 96x16, solid lawn-green bar
- nine bare icons, object only, no plate, no frame: gold coin, blue diamond, yellow lightning, music note, speaker, globe, play triangle, plus sign, minus sign
- tab normal 160x80, cream, pale wood rim, empty
- tab selected 160x80, lawn green, empty
- small primary button 80x80, lawn green square, empty
- small secondary button 80x80, cream square, pale wood rim, empty
- stepper number plate 128x80, cream inset, pale wood rim, no digits

[LAYOUT]
1024x1024 canvas. Do not enlarge widgets to fill the sheet. Every piece is isolated.
Left column: the five wide buttons stacked, with a clear gap between each.
Right side, kept far from the buttons:
1. title plank alone at the top
2. a wide empty gap, then the panel below it. The plank and the panel must not touch
3. item slot and selection frame to the right of the panel, each separated
4. close and help further right, separated
5. tab normal, then a large empty gap at least 80 pixels wide, then tab selected. The two tabs must not touch or overlap
6. stepper plate, then the two small squares, all separated
7. slider track, slider thumb, and progress fill as three objects that do not touch
8. the nine icons in one bottom row, each object floating with space around it, no squares behind them

[BACKGROUND]
flat warm cream, one solid color.

[RESTRICTIONS]
no words, no digits, no labels, no watermark, no animals, no people, no buildings, no map. No overlapping widgets. The awning must not be attached to the panel. Icons have no backing plates.
